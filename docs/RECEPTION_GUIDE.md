# Reception quick start

- The schedule and dispatch boards show appointments, travel status, and client contact details needed to run the day.
- You can record a cash payment or an e-Transfer that you have actually checked, when your access allows it. A client's "I sent the transfer" report does not change the balance.
- Insurance decisions are recorded separately from money received.
- Clinical notes are not on the reception screens. If you need a safety flag, the precautions view shows allergies and contraindications only.
- You do not verify practitioner credentials or refund a card payment. Those stay with the owner.
- Promotional messages are not sent from the reception workspace.
